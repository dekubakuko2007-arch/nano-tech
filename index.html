02 / HACKATHON STUDIO

Make your solution stand up to questions.

Support for the thinking and communication behind hackathon work. No guaranteed wins—just more rigorous problem framing, build plans and presentation.

01

Problem statement analysis

Clarify the target user, context, constraints, assumptions, impact and what the judges or stakeholders are really asking.

↗
02

Solution architecture & planning

Define scope, workflow, technology choices, system architecture, build milestones and validation strategy.

↗
03

Pitch decks & presentation

Craft a coherent story with problem evidence, solution, prototype, implementation, feasibility, differentiation and next steps.

↗
04

Word documents & reports

03 / METHOD

Four steps.
One real problem.

Our guiding process for making project work more deliberate. Adapted to each challenge rather than a fixed ritual.

STEP 01

Listen

Understand the brief, people, needs and constraints.

STEP 02

Frame

Find the core problem and define a meaningful scope.

STEP 03

Build & test

Prototype, compare, validate and refine the direction.

STEP 04

Explain

Make the reasoning, evidence and limits understandable.
