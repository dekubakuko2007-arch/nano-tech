HOW WE APPROACH A PROBLEM

Less guesswork.
More deliberate work.

Good outcomes start with understanding the brief and the people affected. A solution should be explainable, buildable and honest about what remains untested.

  1. 01UnderstandClarify needs, constraints and what success actually means.
  2. 02DefineIdentify the real problem and set a sensible scope.
  3. 03Plan & prototypeCompare approaches, build a testable direction and learn.
  4. 04CommunicatePresent evidence, decisions, limits and next steps clearly.
