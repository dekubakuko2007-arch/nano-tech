EVENT FORMATS

Experiences that give
students a reason to build.

Formats adapted to the campus, audience, schedule and resources—with practical activities rather than passive sessions alone.

FORMAT / 01

Technical workshops

Focused practical sessions introducing tools, workflows and concepts with guided activities.

FORMAT / 02

Hackathons

Team problem-solving with milestones, mentoring, testing and presentations.

FORMAT / 03

Project showcases

Demo spaces where students explain the challenge, build and lessons.

FORMAT / 04

Tech competitions

Structured challenges with clear rules, transparent evaluation and feedback.

FORMAT / 05

Talks & panels

Sessions connecting students with ideas, perspectives and career insights.

FORMAT / 06

Campus collaboration

Joint initiatives with departments, clubs, student teams and institutions.
